# Week 10｜性騷擾 (Sexual Harassment)

## ESP 學習目標 (ESP ILOs)

Students will be able to:

Use key terms such as consent（同意）, boundary（界線）, harassment（騷擾）, and power imbalance（權力不對等） correctly.
Describe an incident using three parts: physical（身體）, emotional（情緒）, and power（權力）.
Explain basic institutional responses, such as reporting（通報）, support（支持）, investigation（調查）, and protection（保護）.

## 關鍵字與反思範例 (Vocabulary & Usage)

關鍵字 (Key Vocabulary)	專業反思範例 (Example)

Power Imbalance（權力不對等）:
A situation where one person has more power or influence than another.
Accountability（責任承擔）:
Being responsible for your actions and results.
Ex: We must ask institutions to take more accountability.
Consent（同意）:
Permission or agreement for something to happen.


## TEEMI 口說練習準備 (Final TEEMI Prep)

Ready to talk to TEEMI? Use these simple frames:

Making a Claim（提出觀點）
I believe institutional responsibility is important to prevent harm.
Describing a Case（描述案例）
In this case, the power imbalance made real consent difficult.
Proposing Action（提出行動）
If we focus on power, not self-blame, we can build a safer culture.
