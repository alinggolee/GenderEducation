# Week 12｜理解數位性別暴力 (Understanding Digital Gender-Based Violence)

## 數位傷害是如何產生的？ (The Anatomy of Digital Harm)

Digital harm is not only caused by bad people.
數位傷害不只是由「壞人」造成的。

It is also caused by how digital platforms are designed.
它也來自於數位平台的設計方式。

## Platforms and Algorithms平台與演算法： 
Digital platforms want you to keep scrolling.
數位平台希望你一直滑下去。

Their systems show content that gets many likes, comments, and shares.
它們的系統會優先顯示按讚、留言與分享多的內容。

Because of this, shocking or harmful gender content often gets more views.
因此，聳動或有害的性別內容常常獲得更多觀看。

Example:
A video that makes fun of girls or boys may get many likes.
嘲笑女生或男生的影片可能會得到很多按讚。

Then the platform shows it to more people.
接著平台會把它推給更多人。

So the problem becomes bigger.
於是問題就被放大了。



## Unequal Impact不平等的影響
Digital violence does not affect everyone in the same way.
數位暴力對每個人的影響並不相同。

Some groups are hurt more than others.
有些群體受到的傷害更大。

This is because society is not equal.
這是因為社會本身並不平等。

Example:
例子：

Girls, LGBTQ+ people, or people who look different may receive more online attacks.
女性、LGBTQ+族群或外貌不同的人，可能會遭受更多網路攻擊。

This shows that online problems come from real-life inequality.
這顯示網路上的問題來自現實生活中的不平等。

## Discussion Questions

### The Digital Mirror數位鏡像
Have you noticed that your “For You” page or feed can change how you think about gender roles?
你是否注意到，你的「推薦頁」或動態牆會影響你對性別角色的看法？

What you see online can slowly shape what you think is normal.
你在網路上看到的內容，會慢慢影響你認為「正常」是什麼。

Example:
例子：

If you often see videos showing girls caring about beauty, you may start to think this is what girls should do.
如果你常看到女生注重外表的影片，你可能會開始覺得這是女生應該做的事。

If you see boys always being strong or not showing feelings, you may think this is “normal” for boys.
如果你看到男生總是很強、不表達情緒，你可能會覺得這對男生來說是「正常的」。

### Platform Accountability平台責任？ 
Who is more responsible for online safety?
誰對網路安全負有更多責任？

The user who needs to be careful, or the platform that shows the content?
是需要小心的使用者，還是提供內容的平台？

Is it your job to protect yourself, or the platform’s job to protect you?
是你要保護自己，還是平台應該保護你？

Example:
例子：

If someone sees harmful content online:
如果有人在網路上看到有害內容：

Should the user just block it and be careful?
使用者只需要封鎖並小心嗎？

Or should the platform stop showing this content in the first place?
還是平台應該一開始就不要讓這些內容出現？

“I think the user is more responsible because…”
「我認為使用者責任比較大，因為……」

“I think the platform is more responsible because…”
「我認為平台責任比較大，因為……」


### Structural Change結構性改變
Instead of only telling people to “block and report,” how can we change the system itself?
除了只告訴人們「封鎖與檢舉」，我們如何改變整個系統？

Don’t just fix the problem — change the system.
不要只解決問題，要改變系統。

Why is the system allowing this to happen?
為什麼這個系統會讓這些事情發生？

Example:

Now we tell users: “Block the person” or “Report the content.”
現在我們會告訴使用者：「封鎖這個人」或「檢舉內容」。

But we can also ask:
但我們也可以問：

Why does this harmful content appear again and again?
為什麼這些有害內容一再出現？

Why doesn’t the platform stop it earlier?
為什麼平台沒有更早阻止它？

What should platforms change to make the internet safer?
平台應該做哪些改變，讓網路更安全？

What rules should be added?
應該增加哪些規則？

## 結語 (Closing Reflection)

We need systemic change, not just personal protection.
我們需要的是系統性的改變，而不只是個人的自我保護。
