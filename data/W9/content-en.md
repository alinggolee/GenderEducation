## Living Traditions 
Tradition is not a static relic. It is a living standard that we can "reinterpret" to fit modern values. We are not just managed by structure; we help it evolve.

## Formation of Identity

The Truku identity shifted  from scattered clan definitions to a unified (統一的) group consciousness (群體意識) due to environmental changes and relocation.

## Closing: How Justice Lives
