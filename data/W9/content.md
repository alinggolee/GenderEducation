## The Dynamic Balance of Agency

Social rules (社會規範) can sometimes feel like a "cage"(牢籠); however, when rules fade (消失、淡化), people may experience a loss of standards. Today, we explore the "dynamic balance" between individuals and systems.

## Living Traditions (活的傳統)
Tradition (傳統) is not a static (靜止的) relic (遺物). It is a living standard that we can "reinterpret" (重新詮釋) to fit (符合) modern values. We are not just managed by structure (結構); we help it evolve (演變、發展).


## Formation (形成) of Identity (認同)

The Truku identity shifted  from scattered (分散的) clan (家族／氏族) definitions (定義) to a unified (統一的) group consciousness (群體意識) due to (由於) environmental changes (環境改變) and relocation (遷移).

### The Fading(消逝的) Voice of the Leader:

Traditional leaders (Bukun) once managed harmony and external relations (對外關係). However, modern administrative systems (現代行政制度) have shifted this authority (權威).

  - Think about the elders in your family. When their "authority" shifts because the world has changed, does that make you feel liberated, or a little sad for the loss of tradition? How can we find our own voice while still respecting our roots?

## Closing: How Justice Lives

Culture (文化) is not fixed. It changes over time (隨時間改變) and across generations (跨世代). Each of us is part of this change. When we understand how social rules work, we begin to have choices. We can choose to keep traditions , change them, or create new meanings.
